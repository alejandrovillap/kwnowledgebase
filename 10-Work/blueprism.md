# Blueprism — RPA Overview

## What is RPA?

Robotic process automation (RPA) is the practice of automating routine business processes with "software robots" that perform tasks automatically. These tasks include transaction processing, IT management, and automated online assistants. Software robots can replace humans for routine, repetitive tasks.

## Benefits of RPA

- Better customer service
- Ensuring business operations comply with regulations and standards
- Completing processes much more rapidly
- Improved efficiency by digitizing and auditing process data
- Cost savings for manual and repetitive tasks
- Enabling employees to be more productive

## Applications by Industry

**Customer Service:** Automating contact-center tasks — verifying e-signatures, uploading scanned documents, verifying information for automatic approvals or rejections.

**Accounting:** General accounting, operational accounting, transactional reporting, and budgeting.

**Financial Services:** Foreign exchange payments, account openings/closings, audit request management, insurance claim processing.

**Healthcare:** Patient records, claims, customer support, account management, billing, reporting, and analytics.

**Human Resources:** Onboarding and offboarding, updating employee information, processing timesheet submissions.
