# Post-Deployment

## AI Product Life Cycle

Deployment is just the beginning of a longer journey. MLOps is crucial for ensuring that your model delivers value over time. Imagine deploying a model like launching a ship; continuous monitoring and maintenance are required to perform well. This includes:

- **Maintaining Model Performance:** The real world changes constantly, affecting your model's data leading to "data drift" and "concept drift." MLOps helps by setting up systems to monitor these changes and alert you when the model needs retraining to stay accurate and relevant.
- **Proactive Monitoring:** Beyond traditional application monitoring, MLOps includes checks for machine learning-specific issues, such as biases or potentially harmful model outputs. This proactive approach helps prevent issues before they affect users or tarnish our reputation.
- **Continuous Improvement:** MLOps facilitates ongoing model training with new data to keep it effective. This could include automatic triggers for retraining when performance drops, as well as methods to detect and prevent fraud.
- **Feedback Loop:** Creating feedback loops is essential. Insights from the model's real-world performance should inform future development and iterations. This helps refine the model for better performance across different customer segments and ensures responsible AI practices by monitoring and correcting biased outputs.

By embracing MLOps, we ensure our models are not just temporary solutions but long-term assets. This journey involves continuous learning and improvement, helping us confidently navigate the evolving landscape of AI. Remember, MLOps is not a destination but a continuous process of enhancing and refining our machine learning models.
