# Integrated Consulting

The Integrated Consulting Framework is part of EPAM Consulting Offering. EPAM Continuum has been established as a value creation network (VCN) within EPAM and acts as a front-end for consulting offerings.

## Components

- **Process**: The "end to end" delivery process.
- **Method Blocks**: Component parts of the framework.
- **Workstreams**: Provides the structure within which teams need to work.

## Steps

1. **Frame** — Define the key goals across the team.
2. **Investigate** — Study "as-is" state and reveal trends.
3. **Envision** — Envision the future.
4. **Evaluate & Define** — Crystalize the target "to-be" vision.
5. **Design & Develop** — Prepare for the delivery.
6. **Launch & Scale** — Work on product and content delivery.
7. **Operate & Optimize** — Ongoing projects support & adjustments.
