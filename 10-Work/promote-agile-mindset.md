# Promote Agile Mindset and Values

## The Agile Iceberg

The Agile iceberg represents the four layers of Agile adoption, from the most visible (top) to the foundational (bottom):

| Level | Layer | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Practices** | The visible tools and ceremonies (Sprints, Standups, Retrospectives) |
| 3 | **Principles** | The 12 Agile Manifesto principles guiding behavior |
| 2 | **Values** | The 4 core Agile values (individuals over processes, etc.) |
| 1 | **Mindset** | The deepest layer — how you fundamentally think about work and change |

The iceberg metaphor highlights that most people see only the practices (above the waterline), while the mindset beneath the surface is what truly drives lasting agility.

## Becoming Agile

Four stages of Agile adoption and internalization:

1. **Conceptualize**: Understand Agile — learn what it is, what the values and principles mean
2. **Internalize**: Commit to it — make a conscious decision to adopt the mindset, not just the practices
3. **Practice**: Its who you are — Agile is no longer something you do, it's how you work
4. **Radiate**: Share it and demonstrate it — model the mindset for others, coach teams, spread culture

## Leading by Example

How to model Agile behaviors and mindset:

- **Values** — You live Agile values (not just talk about them)
- **Ethics** — You make ethical considerations in every decision
- **Actions** — You actually are Agile (not just compliant with a process)
