# Gen AI for Personal Productivity — Manager's Perspective

Managers act as "information pumps" — navigating information flows, conducting meetings and 1:1s, reviewing artifacts, weighing options, making decisions. Gen AI tools can help significantly across these areas.

## Important Caveats

- AI chatbots draft artifacts and save time on boilerplate — but always require review and work on the final document.
- Without full context (e.g., no RAG connection to project data), don't expect AI to have information specific to your project.
- Reassuring-sounding replies may not be grounded in reality — verify before copying.
- Hallucinations and accuracy challenges remain even when external connections exist.

## Common Use Cases for PM/DM

### 1.1 Ideation
- **Methodology selection** — Scrum/Kanban/Scaled Agile/custom
- **Delivery model selection** — engagement and commercial models
- **Development process optimization** — CI/CD, code metrics, TDD guidance

### 1.2 People Management
- **Feedback writing** — draft performance reviews from key-point notes
- **Recognition** — create "thank you" messages for achievements
- **Onboarding** — draft plans, checklists, and training materials for newcomers
- **Stuck on a people challenge?** — AI can suggest frameworks for motivation, conflict resolution

### 1.3 Communication
- **Meeting planning** — generate solid meeting agendas
- **Meeting summaries** — key points, decisions, action items
- **Email drafting** — draft communications adapted to recipient and tone
- **Summarize key findings** — extract facts, ideas, action items from long documents
- **Real-time translation** — break down language barriers for global teams
- **Format conversion** — convert documents to presentations and vice versa
- **Negotiations** — guide negotiation tactics and preparation

### 1.4 Project Management
- SOW / documentation creation
- Project schedule management
- Resource management
- Cost management and budgeting
- Risk management (AI can draft risk treatment strategies)
- Problem solving — frameworks for decision-making, brainstorming, resolution strategies
- Metrics, reporting, and data analytics

### 1.5 Strategic Planning
- Draft Vision and Mission statements
- Template structure for Account Development Plans
- SWOT analysis guidance and data analysis
- Scenario modeling — generate scenarios, predict outcomes, assess risks
- Goal setting — analyze past performance to suggest realistic targets
- Forecasting — predict future trends or business environment changes
- Benchmarking — compare performance against industry benchmarks
- Learning & Development — identify skill gaps and recommend training programs

### 1.6 Client Relationship
- Client feedback analysis — identify trends, issues, opportunities
- Predictive analytics — predict client behavior based on past data
- Relationship management — track interactions, improve CSAT, retain clients
- Conflict resolution strategies based on past successful resolutions
- Stakeholder classification and prioritization

### 1.7 Architecture, Technologies, and Tools
- Architecture design and optimization recommendations
- Technology selection based on project requirements
- Code review — identify errors, bugs, improvement areas
- Technical documentation generation for existing code
- Education — help non-technical PMs understand architecture concepts
- Security best practices and vulnerability identification
- Technical debt strategies

### 1.8 Innovation & Improvements
- Idea generation from data analysis
- Rapid prototyping
- Market and competitive analysis
- Cost-benefit analysis for new ideas
- Quick feedback loop on new ideas without prototypes
- Case study creation and polishing

### 1.9 Time Management
- Meeting preparation briefs
- Task prioritization by urgency, importance, and effort
- Time tracking insights
- Personalized reminders for tasks and deadlines
- Workflow optimization — identify bottlenecks
- Work-life balance — suggest when to take breaks to avoid burnout
