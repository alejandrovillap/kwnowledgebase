# Foster an Environment of Continuous Improvement

## Leading by Example

How do you model Agile behaviors and mindset as a leader?

- **Values** — You live Agile values. Continuous improvement starts with the leader genuinely embracing the principles, not just advocating for them.
- **Ethics** — You make ethical considerations. Sustainable improvement requires decisions that are honest, transparent, and fair to the team.
- **Actions** — You actually are Agile. Behavior is the proof. The team takes its cues from what the leader does, not what they say.

Fostering continuous improvement is not a process intervention — it is a cultural posture that must be demonstrated daily through concrete behaviors, prioritization decisions, and how failure is treated.
