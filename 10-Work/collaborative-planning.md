# Collaborative Planning

## Agile Ceremonies

- **Daily coordination meeting**
  - What did I complete yesterday?
  - What am I planning to complete today?
  - Impediments, risks, problems
- **Sprint planning**
- **Demos**
- **Retrospectives**

Business people and developers must work together daily throughout the project. Reviewing at the most efficient point is key to keeping alignment.

## Part of the Process

- Errors
- Omissions
- Shortcomings

## Establish a Culture of Collaboration

- Frequent meetings
- Iterative feedback
- Use collaborative tools

## Transparent Process

- Share process
- Open backlog
- **Radical transparency**

## Define Roles and Responsibilities

- Client representative
- Agile roles

## Plan Collaboratively

- Joint planning
- User stories
- Test criteria

All of this creates **trust and rapport** between the business and the delivery team.
