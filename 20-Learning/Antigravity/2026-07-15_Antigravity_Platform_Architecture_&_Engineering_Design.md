## Platform Architecture Overview

The architecture centers on two primary actor layers: an **Editor Soul** and a **Human designs** role, both connected through a **Software Soul** bracket to the core platform.

### Platform Components

The **Platform** node branches into the following tools and environments:

- **Cursor**
- **Claude**
- Other (third-party integrations)
- **Agentic Editor Soul**
- **IDE**
- **Citi**

---

## Design & Engineering Philosophy

> **Designing + discussing** engineering decisions for re-use across all current and future partners.

Key design surfaces include:

- **Paper** ← UI/UX redesign
- **API & flow** — collage-style composition
- **Security infrastructure**

---

## Security & Prompt Engineering

The security infrastructure feeds directly into prompt design, governed by three properties:

| Property | Description |
|---|---|
| **Opacity** | Prompts remain non-transparent to external actors |
| **Robustness** | Resilience across varied inputs and edge cases |
| **Simplicity** | Minimal, maintainable prompt surfaces |

---

## Open Option Omnipresent (MOO)

> **MOO** is the "north star" coordination model — simultaneously delegating and coordinating without manual debugging.

Key properties:

- Simultaneously **delegating** and **coordinating**
- **No debugging** required at runtime
- Acts as the **north star** for architectural decisions
- Promises resolution of **context fragmentation**

---

## Production-Grade Specification

A full production specification covers four areas:

1. **Full solution design**
2. **Background → help → from demos**
3. **Visual aids and iteration opinions**
4. **Contracts**, defined along three axes:
   - *Correct* — expected behavior
   - *Failure* — defined failure modes
   - *Long term* — sustained delivery guarantees

> → **Always deliver outcomes**

---

## Creating: Output Layer

The final stage translates specification into creation:

- **Definition of ideas**
- **Resource mapping**
