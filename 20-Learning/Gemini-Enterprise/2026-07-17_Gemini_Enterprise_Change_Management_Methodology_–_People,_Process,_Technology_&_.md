## The Three Dimensions of Every Deployment

> Every deployment operates across three interconnected dimensions: **People**, **Process**, and **Technology**. When all three align, true **work transformation** occurs.

| Dimension | Focus |
|---|---|
| **People** | All activities needed to bring everyone on the journey — engaging sponsors, training, communications, and celebrating successes |
| **Process** | Understanding day-to-day business processes and creating opportunities for improvement through Gemini Enterprise |
| **Technology** | Achieving a smooth transition with connected data and the right security and policies in place |

---

## Why Change Management Matters

### People Don't Like Change

Most people are naturally resistant to change. According to **Everett Rogers**, only **16% of people** adapt easily to change — meaning the remaining **84% will need active support** along the way.

Fortunately, people react in fairly predictable ways to change, even when it is expected. Eventually they accept it, begin experimenting, and integrate it into their lives.

> **Goal of change management:** Remove fear and uncertainty through well-defined communication, alleviate frustration through training, and help users embrace the change.

### What Effective Change Management Looks Like

- **Eliminate fear, uncertainty, and doubt** — address fears and misconceptions directly
- **Study their business processes** — understand challenges and develop targeted solutions
- **Train them on the technology** — ensure users get the most out of Gemini Enterprise

### Start Small, Then Spread Through Success

One proven approach is to **win over a few key people first**, then expand engagement across the organization — using those original wins as **case studies** to build broader momentum.

## The Change Journey

> Change management is **not an isolated process** — it begins within the sales cycle and continues throughout delivery.

The **Gemini Enterprise Change Management Methodology** addresses the challenges of change through a structured journey. It begins with **Preparation**, followed by four key phases:

1. **Excite**
2. **Activate**
3. **Expand**
4. **Transform**

Each phase is crucial for long-term customer success. Do not discount early preparation and communication, or the value of analyzing success metrics long after go-live.

---

## Phase 0: Prepare

Preparation is the foundation of the entire change management journey.

- Review project resources available in the **Partner Advantage Portal** and **Delivery Navigator**
- Identify available resources to support your customer's organization along the journey
- Plan the following phases of engagement

*The initial Excite phase focuses on identifying stakeholders, defining vision, scope, and rollout strategy, setting up the adoption team, and deploying a minimum viable Gemini Enterprise solution.*

---

## Phase 1: Excite

**Primary actions:**
- Engage sponsors and gain sponsorship
- Align sponsors and build excitement for the change

**Customer-focused activities:**
- Analyze organizational needs
- Identify **Gemini Enterprise use cases** and determine which to tackle first
- Identify user groups and outline the scope of work
- Assess organizational impacts

**Key focus areas:**
- Engage sponsors
- Analyze organizational needs

---

## Phase 2: Activate

> This phase sees the **largest deployment rollout and user adoption** — actions taken here provide the critical foundation for all following phases.

### Day 1 Value

From Day 1, customers experience immediate value through:

- An **intuitive, chat-based experience** to interact with the system
- **Quick insights from documents** — immediate understanding from existing data
- Ability to **create and understand rich media**, expanding content types
- **No-code agent design** — empowering users to build solutions without deep technical expertise

### Activate Activities

- Demonstrate the value unlocked from day one
- Leverage **out-of-the-box capabilities** and initial **data connectors**
- Prototype and iterate on useful queries to share across the organization
- Develop **no-code agents**
- Explore possibilities with **advanced agents and connectors**

**Key focus areas:**
- Lead with Day 1 value
- Connect data
- Develop starter no-code agents

---

## Phase 3: Expand

> The Expand phase drives **sustained engagement** by integrating new data sources and more complex agents, while fostering **AI fluency** across the organization.

### Expand Activities

- Communicate **top-down vision** and **bottom-up successes** to grow adoption
- Manage support operations
- Promote adoption and prioritize transformation
- Develop a **communications plan**
- Combine high-level strategic planning with practical input from the teams who will use the technology

### Customer Capabilities Unlocked

- **Build simple agents** — further increasing investment value
- **Securely build and govern agents** — ensuring operation within organizational guidelines
- **Scale agents with confidence** — expanding reach and impact
- **Find answers across enterprise data** — centralizing and streamlining information access
- **Connect agents to existing work apps** — embedding AI directly into current workflows

**Key focus area:**
- Communicate vision and success

---

## Phase 4: Transform

> The Transform phase champions **continuous innovation**, scales output, and reimagines how work gets done — successfully transforming the workforce.

### Transform Activities

- Plan and run **training workshops**
- Measure adoption and promote success stories
- Provide **self-help resources**
- Champion innovation initiatives
- Train on **new releases**
- Evaluate and report on adoption metrics

### Advanced Capabilities Unlocked

- **Develop advanced agents and connectors**
- **Fully integrate AI** into daily tools and workflows — making it indispensable
- **Orchestrate multiple agents** for complex, sophisticated tasks
- **Create new revenue with AI** — opening new business opportunities
- **Deliver personalized AI agents for all** — tailoring solutions to individual needs across the organization

**Key focus areas:**
- Train for ongoing adoption
- Evaluate adoption metrics
