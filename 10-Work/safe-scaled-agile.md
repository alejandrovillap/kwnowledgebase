# SAFe — Scaled Agile Framework

SAFe is a framework for scaling agile methodologies at the enterprise level, integrating Lean, Agile, and DevOps into a comprehensive operating system. Created by Dean Leffingwell. SAFe 6.0 is the current version, practiced in 20,000+ companies globally.

## The 4 Configurations

| Configuration | Levels Included | Ideal Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| Essential SAFe | Team + Program | Organizations starting with 1-2 ARTs |
| Large Solution SAFe | Team + Program + Large Solution | Complex solutions requiring multiple coordinated ARTs |
| Portfolio SAFe | Team + Program + Portfolio | Corporations with multiple products needing strategic alignment |
| Full SAFe | All levels | Global organizations with multiple value streams and complex systems |

## The 7 Organizational Levels

1. **Team** — agile teams (Scrum, Kanban, XP) delivering value increments
2. **Program** — Agile Release Train (ART) coordinating 5-12 teams (50-125 people)
3. **Large Solution** — multiple ARTs for complex enterprise solutions
4. **Portfolio** — Lean Portfolio Management aligning strategy with execution
5. **Value Stream** — end-to-end flow connecting multiple portfolios
6. **Enterprise** — complete organizational agility
7. **Foundation** — Lean-Agile leadership, culture, and competencies

## The 7 Business Agility Competencies

1. Lean-Agile Leadership
2. Technical and Team Agility
3. Agile Product Delivery
4. Enterprise Solution Delivery
5. Lean Portfolio Management
6. Organizational Agility
7. Continuous Learning Culture

## Key Roles by Level

**Team Level:** Scrum Master (facilitates, removes impediments), Product Owner (manages backlog, prioritizes), Team Members (design, develop, test).

**Program Level (ART):**
- **Release Train Engineer (RTE)** — "Scrum Master of the train"; facilitates coordination of 5-12 teams, manages PI Planning, resolves cross-team impediments
- **Product Manager** — "PO of the program"; defines product vision, prioritizes Features, creates roadmap
- **System Architect/Engineer** — defines technical architecture, guides technical decisions
- **Business Owners** — executives responsible for ROI, connect program to strategy

**Portfolio Level:**
- **Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)** — defines strategy, assigns budgets, manages value flow
- **Enterprise Architect** — defines corporate architecture, aligns ARTs
- **Epic Owners** — responsible for specific Epics, define business case and KPIs

## Planning Cycles

**Program Increment (PI):** Main SAFe rhythm. Duration: **8-12 weeks** (typically 10), containing 5-6 iterations.

**PI Planning (2 days every 10 weeks):**
- Day 1: Product vision, business objectives, Feature identification, dependency analysis
- Day 2: Team planning, risk identification, plan review, train commitment
- Deliverables: PI objectives, delivery plan, identified risks, train commitment

**Iteration (Sprint) — 2 weeks:** Sprint Planning → Daily Stand-up → Iteration Review → Retrospective.

**System Demo:** Integration demo at the end of each iteration.

**Inspect & Adapt (end of PI):** Full system demo + PI metrics (predictability, velocity, quality) + Problem Solving Workshop.

## When to Use Each Configuration

| Configuration | When | Implementation Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Essential SAFe | 1-2 ARTs, first adoption | 3-6 months |
| Large Solution SAFe | 3+ ARTs, complex solution | 6-12 months |
| Portfolio SAFe | Multiple products, strategic alignment needed | 9-18 months |
| Full SAFe | Global organization, multiple value streams | 12-24 months |

## Implementation Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Scope & Preparation (Months 1-3):** Executive commitment, certify SPCs, train leaders, form transformation coalition.

**Phase 2 — Initial Launch (Months 3-6):** Identify value streams and ARTs, create implementation plan, train roles, launch first ART.

**Phase 3 — Scaling (Months 6-18):** Train business owners, launch more ARTs, extend to Portfolio, sustain and improve.

## Quantifiable Benefits

- 30-50% reduction in time-to-market
- 20-40% improvement in team productivity
- 50-70% increase in quality (defect reduction)
- 25-35% improvement in customer satisfaction
- ROI of 3:1 to 5:1 in first 18-24 months
- Breakeven: 12-18 months; ROI at 3 years: 250-400%
