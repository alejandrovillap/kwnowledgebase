# AI Product Life Cycle Overview

The AI Product Life Cycle is an iterative process with four key phases, each vital for ensuring the product meets its intended goals and delivers value to users.

## Four Phases

**AI Product Ideation:** Brainstorming and conceptualizing the AI product. Generating ideas, gathering input from stakeholders, and documenting a product development roadmap.

**AI Product Prototyping:** Teams experiment with conceptualized ideas. Set up environments for prototype development, explore data, validate AI/ML technologies, and develop an initial version for feedback.

**AI Product Productionization:** Refining the product and preparing it for deployment. Automating pipelines, addressing performance and security issues, and rolling out the first production version.

**AI Product Operation:** Once live, ensuring continuous monitoring, support, and improvement based on user feedback and data analysis.

## Three Development Approaches

### Platform-Centric Approach
Ideal for organizations with a strong background in data science. Focuses on creating a robust MLOps platform handling everything from data management to model deployment. Requires significant initial investment but offers a highly efficient, scalable system for mature clients looking to expand AI capabilities.

### Use Case-Centric Approach
Best for organizations new to data science or with limited resources. Targets specific, high-impact use cases by implementing tailored MLOps pipelines for each. Requires less initial investment and provides a quicker ROI.

### Hybrid Approach
Combines platform-centric and use-case-driven strategies. Enables organizations to build a central MLOps platform while addressing specific use cases simultaneously — quick impacts through targeted projects with focus on long-term benefits.
