# AI Product Framing

Creating a successful AI product requires collaboration between your team and the client's subject matter experts (SMEs). The process has four key phases.

## Brainstorming

Start with a collaborative brainstorming session with your AI specialists and client SMEs. Clients often have existing ideas or prototypes that can serve as a foundation. Focus on specific industry challenges and encourage open discussion — this uncovers pain points and fosters empathy, especially if there's a history of failed projects. Set clear goals and time limits to keep it productive.

## Prioritizing Ideas

After generating a range of ideas, prioritize them based on feasibility. SMEs provide insights into data availability and potential privacy concerns. Your AI team assesses which ideas suit ML implementation. Be honest about whether a simpler solution might be more effective than complex AI.

## Prototyping & Validation

Select the most promising ideas and move to rapid prototyping. Use the Crawl-Walk-Run approach to quickly develop a basic AI product. Get this prototype into the hands of client SMEs for feedback — their insights are crucial for ensuring the product meets their needs.

## Refining the Model

Use SME feedback to refine the AI product. This iterative process ensures the final product addresses specific challenges and leverages industry knowledge.

## Why Engaging SMEs Early Matters

**Understanding the Language:** SMEs are fluent in industry-specific terminology and can help identify the most relevant data points for training. In a legal context, an SME can differentiate between "high-priority" and "urgent" cases, ensuring the AI model interprets data accurately.

**Bridging the Knowledge Gap:** SMEs identify relevant data sources and provide context for model outputs. If an AI predicts case success rates, an SME explains the factors influencing that prediction.

**Early Collaboration:** Engaging SMEs early allows course corrections before significant investments are made. Their insights help identify biases in data or limitations in the chosen model.

**Co-Piloting Development:** Collaboration ensures the AI product is tailored to unique needs. Product Owners play a vital role in consolidating knowledge and keeping the project on track — ideally with some industry background.
